Why Go Solar

Everything You Gain by Going Solar

Solar power isn't just good for the environment — it's one of the smartest financial decisions a homeowner can make.

💰

Dramatically Lower Bills

Most of our customers slash their electricity bills by 70–90%. Excess energy is sold back to the grid through net metering, putting cash back in your pocket.

🌱

Reduce Carbon Footprint

A typical residential solar system offsets 3–4 tonnes of CO₂ per year — equivalent to planting over 100 trees annually. Go green without compromise.

🏠

Increase Property Value

Homes with solar systems sell for an average of 4% more. It's an upgrade that pays for itself and then continues delivering returns for decades.

🔋

Energy Independence

Paired with battery storage, you gain true energy independence — power that keeps your home running even during grid outages and peak demand periods.

🛡️

25-Year Warranty

Our Tier 1 panels come with a full 25-year performance warranty. We also offer 10-year workmanship guarantees on all our installations.

📊

Tax Credits & Incentives

Take advantage of the 30% Federal Solar Tax Credit plus state-level rebates and SRECs. Our team handles all paperwork to maximise your savings.
